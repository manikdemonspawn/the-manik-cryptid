Realm Index

The visual thresholds are the main experience. This page exists so nobody has to solve a haunted forest puzzle just to navigate the site.

Fairy-Tale / Fantasy Region

The fairy-tale side of the Forest: carved-trunk and standing-stone thresholds that read like storybook doors.

The Library

Decontamination threshold. Writing, soundscapes, apps, art, multimedia, and special collections.

The Museum

Stonehenge threshold. History, lineage, provenance, credentials, milestones, and core artifacts.

Native-Inspired Edge

The Forest Path runs from the fairy-tale side through the central transition to the native-inspired edge, where the Kurio Shoppe sits. Cross over and the woods change their mood.

The Kurio Shoppe

Round storefront threshold. Manik-made goods and an affiliate yard sale out back (see the Yard Sale); the Apothekary is upstairs.

The Apothekary

UNDER KONSTRUCTION. It is upstairs inside the Kurio Shoppe, behind a rope at the bottom of the stairs.

Elsewhere in the Forest

The rest of the Forest: protected, practical, and deliberately unfinished thresholds scattered beyond the two main regions.

The Krypt

Coffin or sarcophagus threshold. Protected truth, guarded memory, honored dead, afterlife-belief threads, restricted indexes, and offline-only records.

The Guildhall

Castle-door threshold. Skills, disciplines, capabilities, crafts, portfolio, and guild records.

The Morgue

Clinical threshold. Dead projects, toe tags, death dates, resurrection dates, and ghost artifacts.

The Forge

UNDER KONSTRUCTION. It is a top-level realm; its final purpose and content remain deliberately unclaimed.

Forbidden Library: it is not a Nexus realm entrance. It lives inside the Library behind the hidden bookcase and black door. The Konstruction build contains no adult material and no real age-verification system yet.

Katakombs: use the separate basement portal at the bottom of the Nexus for the legalese-only infrastructure realm. It is not counted among the eight primary realms.

Death, burial & legacy: the Morgue records the deaths — and the Semmaterry™ buries the absolutely dead, tombstone by tombstone.
