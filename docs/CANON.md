# Resurrection Artifact Universe — Website Canon (Construction)

This file records structural rules so future additions do not quietly mutate the universe.

## Core rule

These are **realms**, not rooms and not ordinary navigation pages. Each realm has its own threshold, atmosphere, font identity, and internal logic. The Nexus connects them.

The threshold should communicate the realm before the visitor reads the label. Text labels remain for accessibility and orientation.

## Nexus

- Fairy forest.
- Manik Cryptid visual branding throughout: mushrooms, skulls, hanging curiosities, strange forest objects, carved trunks, roots, mist, etc.
- Cultural zones remain distinct rather than becoming a generic folklore blend.
- Irish / Scottish fairy influence is strongest around the Stonehenge Museum threshold.
- A separate healing / living-apothecary path can sit around the Catacomb area.
- Cherokee and Blackfoot inscriptions or writing must be sourced carefully, verified, and kept distinct from each other.
- Dreamcatcher presence is personal/ambient, not presented as Cherokee or Blackfoot iconography. One medium-to-large piece is preferable to turning the whole zone into the brand motif.
- The living-apothecary forest area may include rue, ginger, thyme, honey, bees/hive imagery, and other botanical details.
- Norse influence sits around the Morgue / Crypt death-memory zone.
- Elder Futhark inscriptions use sound-based transliteration and carry readable translations underneath.
- No active Mausoleum threshold appears while the creator is alive.

## Museum

Threshold: **enormous Stonehenge**.

Stonehenge belongs to the threshold. The interior becomes a real museum rather than a Stonehenge-themed exhibit hall.

Purpose: history, family lore, lineage, identity, provenance, milestones, credentials, and core personal artifacts.

Display hierarchy:

- certificates / credentials = enormous ceremonial frames;
- foundational people, images, events, and core identity pieces = over-the-fireplace scale;
- ordinary important artifacts = normal exhibit scale.

The Museum remembers where the Cryptid came from and what made her.

## Library

Threshold: **decontamination / preservation chamber**.

Scale goal: **Vatican-archive enormous**.

The decontamination threshold is controlled/preservation-focused. The Library interior is old-world archival grandeur rather than a laboratory.

Collections can include:

- books / writing;
- audio / soundscapes;
- apps / interactive work;
- art / images;
- multimedia;
- special collections;
- platform gateways such as Spotify, YouTube, Patreon, app stores, direct distribution, and future storefronts.

The Library is the canonical catalog even when heavyweight media or software lives elsewhere.

Resurrected Library works may wander the stacks like zombies while retaining death and resurrection metadata.

### Forbidden Library

Lives **inside** the Library.

Sequence:

Library → hidden bookcase → black door → Dante inscription → age verification → restricted collections.

Black-door inscription, carved into the door itself:

> ABANDON ALL HOPE, YE WHO ENTER HERE.

The eventual age restriction must protect direct access to restricted material and assets, not only the theatrical entrance.

Restricted collections may include erotica, erotic artwork, trauma-heavy material, graphic horror, disturbing imagery, psychological/extreme work, and other material not intended for minors.

Erotica gets the red shelf: oxblood / wine / deep crimson.

The Forbidden Library is a mature restricted archive, not merely an erotica/porn section.

## Morgue

Threshold: **institutional morgue entry with gurneys**.

Every dead work gets a toe tag with date of death.

A resurrected work retains:

- date of death;
- date resurrected;
- resurrected status.

Resurrected Morgue artifacts may float around like ghosts. Toe-tag history remains attached. Death history is never erased.

Norse death-memory influence can exist around the Morgue without turning the clinical interior into theme-park Viking décor.

## Crypt

Threshold: **coffin / sarcophagus**.

Part of the death-memory region. Stone, burial, memory, the honored dead, and afterlife belief threads belong here.

Norse influences can be present without treating multiple afterlife traditions as interchangeable.

Valhalla may exist as a belief thread but is not framed as the creator's personal destination. The creator's stated lens is survivor rather than warrior.

Other afterlife concepts such as Summerland remain clearly separate traditions.

## Guild Hall

Threshold: **castle door. Full stop.**

Purpose: disciplines, capabilities, crafts, portfolio, skills, worktables, and guild records.

Credentials can cross-reference Museum artifacts instead of being duplicated.

## Curio Shop

Threshold: **round storybook / Hobbit-ish storefront door**, without direct Tolkien imitation.

Main floor: things made by The Manik Cryptid.

Upstairs: Apothecary.

Back door: affiliate yard sale.

### Apothecary

Voice: **The Manik Cryptid herself**, not an abstract universe narrator.

Product model:

- free synopsis / shelf card;
- purpose / meaning;
- context;
- partial ingredients/materials;
- paid full ritual/practice with complete instructions, full list, meanings, preparation/context, and sourcing guidance.

Physical kits are not required. Digital knowledge is the default product.

Herbal / healing / spiritual copy should remain honest about tradition, ritual, symbolism, personal practice, or wellness use and should not promise guaranteed treatment for disease.

### Affiliate Yard Sale

Visitors step out the back door and see a large affiliate disclosure banner **before** they begin browsing.

Affiliate finds remain visibly distinct from Cryptid-made goods.

The yard can use folding tables, crates, mismatched objects, lanterns, fences, and a deliberately more lawless visual style than the shop interior.

## Catacombs

Threshold: **cellar doors**.

The Catacombs are the basement realm. Entry is a descent. Roots and forest give way to stone, tunnels, gates, and low light.

The culturally influenced forest zone belongs around the Catacomb threshold in the Nexus. It is not automatically pasted into the underground interior.

## Future Mausoleum

**Posthumous only. Do not expose an active Nexus portal while the creator is alive.**

When activated:

- mausoleum appears in the Nexus with the creator's name;
- one central sarcophagus;
- carved stone walls;
- inscriptions, names, dates, symbols, Cryptidisms;
- interactive carvings can eventually open memories, recordings, images, writing, or personal explanations.

Purpose:

- Museum = history / provenance.
- Mausoleum = the honest person underneath the mythology.

It remains unmistakably a mausoleum, not a memorial lounge or cozy study.

## Font rule

- Shared body text: readable handwriting feel, not cursive/script.
- Each realm gets its own display/accent font identity.
- Younger users must not be forced to decode cursive for ordinary navigation/body copy.
- Norse layer: Elder Futhark with translation below.
- Elder Futhark transliteration follows sounds rather than pretending modern English letters map one-to-one.
- Cherokee / Blackfoot writing: verified material only and clearly separated.

## Visual-target rule

Build code, layout, interactions, metadata, responsive behavior, and content hierarchy as far as possible **before** generating final visual-target images.

Then generate:

1. one very large full-Nexus visual target with active portal thresholds;
2. one target for each realm / major subrealm as close as possible to intended final appearance.

Use `docs/VISUAL_TARGET_BRIEF.md` as the art-direction source.
