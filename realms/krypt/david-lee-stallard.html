David Lee Stallard

1976 – 2026  •  Olive Hill, Kentucky

Up front, where he belongs

David was my protector and my BFF from back home. That’s the whole of who he was to me, and it was everything. He’s honored here — first stone in the wall — because that’s where he belongs.

The Man

Builder

A certified carpenter who loved building things with his own two hands. He was proud of the work — you could hear it when he talked about it.

Family

A father of three. A grandfather of five. A brother. A friend. Forty-nine years old — he would have turned fifty on October 14, 2026.

What Happened

David was last seen August 1–2, 2026, near Route 2 and Henderson Cemetery Road in Olive Hill, Carter County, Kentucky. On September 12, 2026, his neighbor Christopher Webb was arrested and charged with murder, tampering with physical evidence, and abuse of a corpse.

Charged — not convicted. The court gets the last word on that. This page is about David, not the case.

His Marks

David carried his story on his skin. These were the marks they used to identify him while people were searching:

  • “Marsha” on his neck
  • “13 1/2” on his hand
  • A sword through a heart, with the words “love honor obey,” on his arm
  • A dagger through a heart, with the words “David love Beatrice,” on his arm
  • “FTW” on his arm
  • A solitary playing card with a spider on his bicep

Remembered

He’s not a name in a case file. He was a man who built things, raised a family, and stood up for the people he loved. He’s remembered here, with weight.

— MANIK
