Burial Ground • Absolutely Dead Artifacts

The Semmaterry™

This is the burial ground for ABSOLUTELY DEAD artifacts. The Morgue records the death; the Semmaterry™ keeps the grave. Every burial is permanent on the record — the grave is never erased.

The Tombstones

Every buried artifact gets a tombstone. Each tombstone shows, at minimum:

Name

The artifact's name as it lived.

Creation Date

When the artifact was made.

Burial Date

When it was buried in the Semmaterry™.

Status

DECEASED — or, if it ever walks back out, the resurrection record.

Family Tree

Access to the artifact's genealogy: parents, descendants, and what they became.

If an artifact is ever resurrected from the Semmaterry™, the grave does not disappear — the original tombstone remains part of the history.
